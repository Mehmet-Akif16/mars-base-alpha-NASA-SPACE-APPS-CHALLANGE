# Mars Base Alpha

**A 14-sol Mars base survival game for kids aged 7–12, driven by real NASA data.**

## What it is

The player is the commander of a small Mars base. For 14 sols (Martian days) they keep six systems alive:

| Bar | What it stands for | NASA counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ Power | Heat, light, pumps, radio | Mars surface power: solar arrays and the MMRTG |
| 🫧 Oxygen | Air to breathe | ECLSS life support, MOXIE on Perseverance |
| 💧 Water | Drinking, greenhouse, oxygen | ISS Water Recovery System (98% recovery) |
| 🌱 Food | Greenhouse crops and stored meals | Advanced Life Support, Veggie experiments |
| 🛡️ Shield | Protection from radiation | Curiosity RAD measurements |
| 💚 Morale | Crew stress and teamwork | Human Factors & Behavioral Health, HERA |

Every bar drops a little each sol. Each sol brings a decision card with two choices, and **every choice helps some systems and costs others**. If any bar reaches zero, the mission ends. Reach sol 14 with all six alive and the mission succeeds.

After each decision, an **Info Corner** card explains the real space technology behind it.

### Learning goals

- A space base is an **engineering system**, not just a place to live.
- **Systems thinking:** improving one thing always costs something else.
- Real numbers and real events from NASA, not invented ones.

## How to play

No installation, no internet needed.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Double-click **`index.html`**. It opens in any modern browser.
3. Pick a landing site, read each card, choose, and keep your base alive.

Use the **TR / EN** button in the corner to switch language.

## Gameplay at a glance

1. **Landing site choice** – three real Martian regions, each changes the starting conditions:
   - **Elysium Planitia** – flat, sunny, safe. Low science reward.
   - **Jezero Crater** – ancient river delta, highest science reward. Less sunlight, harsher dust.
   - **Arsia Mons** – giant volcano. Highest power, weakest shield.
2. **14 sols of decisions** – dust storms, solar storms, equipment failures, the first harvest.
3. **Real space weather** – the solar storms in the game follow the real events of **3–16 May 2024**, the strongest geomagnetic storm in about 20 years (an X4.5 flare on sol 4, a Kp 9.0 storm on sol 8).
4. **Mission report** – a star rating and the data sources used.

### Balance testing

The game was balanced by simulating **all 524,288 possible playthroughs**. Random play wins about 44% of the time; careful play wins. All six bars can end the mission, so none of them is decoration.

## NASA data used

| Source | What we used | Where in the game |
|---|---|---|
| [DONKI](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/) – CCMC / Moon to Mars Space Weather Analysis Office | Solar flares (FLR), solar energetic particles (SEP), CMEs, geomagnetic storms (GST), 3–16 May 2024 | Solar storm alerts on sols 4 and 8, weather panel |
| [Mars Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/) | Viking MDIM2.1 global color mosaic | Landing site maps |
| [NASA OSDR](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/) | OSD-895 (EDEN ISS greenhouse biomass), OSD-984 (muscle and cosmic radiation), OSD-1018 (isolation and radiation) | Food, health and morale info cards |
| [RadLab](https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/) (OSDR) | ISS radiation dose measurements (DosTel) | Radiation info cards |
| [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/) | Mars surface photos PIA26680, PIA24264 | Background scenes |

The raw downloads are kept in the [`data/`](data/) folder as proof of source.

## Use of artificial intelligence

We used AI openly and we list exactly where:

- **Code and interface:** written with **Claude** (Anthropic), following the team's design mockups.
- **Story and decision scenarios:** written by our team.
- **Info Corner texts:** drafted with Claude, then every card was fact-checked against NASA sources in a separate step; 13 of the 19 cards were corrected during that check.
- **Balance numbers:** tuned by simulating every possible playthrough, not by guessing.
- **Data:** downloaded directly from the NASA sources listed above. No data was generated by AI.
